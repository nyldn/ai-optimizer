# Code of conduct

We want this project to be useful to people with different tools, experience
levels, identities, and working styles.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Do not harass, threaten, demean, or expose another person's private information.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations
and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The
maintainer will review reports in good faith, protect confidentiality where
practical, and explain any enforcement action to the affected participant.
